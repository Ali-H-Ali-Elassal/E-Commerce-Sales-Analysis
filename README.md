# E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to understand revenue performance, identify top-selling products, and explore sales trends across different countries and product categories.

## Tools Used
- **SQL (BigQuery):** Data querying, filtering, joins, and revenue calculations
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data preparation and organization
- **Tableau:** Data visualization and dashboard development

## Project Objectives
1. Analyze revenue across product categories.
2. Identify the top-selling products.
3. Examine monthly revenue performance.
4. Compare revenue across countries.
5. Explore sales trends over time.

## Dataset
The project includes summarized analysis results covering:
- 26 product categories
- 15 countries
- 94 monthly revenue records

## Key Findings
- Outerwear & Coats generated $347,334.64 in revenue in the analyzed results.
- China generated $928,536.96 in revenue in the analyzed results.
- Sales performance varied across product categories, countries, and time periods.

# Business Insights & Recommendations

### Business Problem
An e-commerce business needs to understand which products and markets generate revenue, how sales change over time, and where to investigate opportunities for growth.

### What the Data Shows
- The analysis covers 26 product categories and 15 countries.
- Outerwear & Coats generated $347,334.64 in revenue in the analyzed results.
- China generated $928,536.96 in revenue in the analyzed results.
- The dashboard compares monthly revenue, top-selling products, geographic performance, and sales trends.

### Business Recommendations
1. **Product strategy:** Investigate the products generating the most revenue and evaluate whether they deserve additional inventory or marketing support.
2. **Geographic strategy:** Review sales performance in China and compare it with other markets to identify potential growth opportunities.
3. **Revenue monitoring:** Track monthly sales trends to identify unusual changes and possible seasonal patterns.
4. **Customer analysis:** Extend the project to study repeat purchases, average order value, and customer retention.

### Limitations
Revenue alone does not measure profitability. Additional information about product costs, marketing expenses, returns, and customer behavior would be needed before recommending specific investments.
